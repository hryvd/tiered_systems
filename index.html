Resume and technical portfolio

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible to participate?

All Computer Engineering and engineering students at Batangas State University Alangilan preparing for internship, OJT, or entry-level technical roles.

What should I prepare for Day 1?

Have a digital copy of your draft resume, project files or demo link for at least one technical project, and your active LinkedIn credentials.

Are certificates of completion issued?

Yes. Attendance is recorded separately for Day 1 (Zoom) and Day 2 (STEER Hub). Certificates of Completion are awarded to students who complete the workshop deliverables.

Can I access the Opportunity Board after the event?

Yes. The board stays permanently online and accessible on desktop and mobile. Your saved star bookmarks persist safely in your browser.

Who is organizing this program?

The Computer Engineering Students' Organization (CURSOR) in collaboration with CIRCUIT 2.0 mentors and the College of Engineering.
